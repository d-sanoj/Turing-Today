# Turing > Today

Turing > Today is a visual history of artificial intelligence, told as a scrollable timeline from the foundations of computation to the current age of frontier models, AI infrastructure, agents, and regulation.

The project follows AI as a long story rather than a single invention. It begins with Alan Turing, early electronic computing, artificial neurons, and the Dartmouth Conference, then moves through symbolic AI, expert systems, AI Winters, statistical machine learning, deep learning, transformers, generative AI, and the modern race around chips, cloud infrastructure, multimodal systems, reasoning models, and AI agents.

## What This Timeline Covers

- The foundations of AI: computation, logic, early neural ideas, and machine intelligence.
- The birth of AI as a field: Dartmouth, LISP, perceptrons, ELIZA, Shakey, and early robotics.
- The rise and limits of symbolic AI: expert systems, rule-based reasoning, and the AI Winters.
- The shift toward learning from data: support vector machines, Bayesian networks, LSTM, CNNs, and big data.
- The deep learning revival: ImageNet, GPUs, AlexNet, ResNet, TensorFlow, PyTorch, and TPUs.
- The foundation model era: transformers, BERT, GPT, Llama, Claude, Gemini, and open-weight models.
- The generative AI boom: GANs, diffusion models, Stable Diffusion, ChatGPT, GPT-4, multimodal AI, and AI video.
- The current frontier: NVIDIA infrastructure, AI agents, reasoning models, DeepSeek-R1, and the EU AI Act.

## People and Organizations

The timeline also includes major researchers, builders, labs, and companies whose work shaped AI history. It highlights figures such as Alan Turing, John McCarthy, Marvin Minsky, Frank Rosenblatt, Judea Pearl, Geoffrey Hinton, Yann LeCun, Fei-Fei Li, Demis Hassabis, Jensen Huang, Sam Altman, Dario Amodei, and many others.

Organizations covered include OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Google DeepMind, NVIDIA, Microsoft, AWS, Meta, IBM, Mistral AI, Hugging Face, SRI International, Stability AI, Runway, CompVis, DeepSeek, and the European Union.
